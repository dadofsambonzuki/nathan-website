## Overview

Where is Bitcoin actually accepted? This talk answers that from the ground up, using the [BTC Map](https://btcmap.org/) dataset rather than exchange volume or opinion.

- What BTC Map is, and how the map stays maintained
- The myths around Bitcoin as money — and why Gresham's law cannot apply to it
- The adoption landscape as it stands: which countries and communities are growing, which have stalled, and how recently each place was verified

Every figure in the deck is generated from the live dataset rather than typed by hand.
