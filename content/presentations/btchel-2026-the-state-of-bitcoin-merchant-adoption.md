## Overview

Where is Bitcoin actually accepted? This talk answers that from the ground up, using the [BTC Map](https://btcmap.org/) dataset rather than exchange volume or opinion. It covers what BTC Map is and how the map gets maintained, walks through some of the myths that surround Bitcoin as money — including why Gresham's law cannot apply to it — and then maps the adoption landscape as it really stands: which countries and communities are growing, which have stalled, and how recently anyone verified the places on the map.

Every figure in the deck is generated from the live dataset rather than typed by hand, so the picture is as current as the map itself.
